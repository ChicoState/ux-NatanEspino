# My Experience With Roblox Support and Trying to Recover a Hacked Account

## Background

A few months ago, I unfortunately lost access to my Roblox account which I used to play Roblox games several years ago. I had been getting mysterious login requests sent to my email from Russia, and so I naturally assumed that the account had been hacked into. I quickly tried to log into my account to see if I could change the password and hopefully not lose the account, but unfortunately my email and password no longer worked. Because of this, I was forced to try and recover my account through Roblox support.

## Experience

Going into this process, my expectation was that Roblox would provide some way to get in contact with a real person at Roblox that could help me recover my account. This was my **mental model** of for the support system, the expectation I had of how the system would work and what it would provide. From a quick google search, I was able to find [this](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313390-My-account-was-hacked-What-do-I-do) page on Roblox's official website which offers some advice on what to do to recover my account. I scrolled down the page to the section which was most relevant to me, "If resetting your password doesn't work". Here, Roblox support suggests to contact them using the first email address connected with the account, and they provide a link to a page where I can contact support.

This is a form which is prompting me to enter information related to my account, like my email, username, and a description of the problem I am having. After entering the username and email, I enter the following description of my problem into the form and clicked continue.

"A few months ago, I received several login requests to my email, informing me that someone from Russia was trying to login to my account. After attempting to login, trying to reset my password, and attempting to receive a one-time login code, I was not able to login to my account. I believe my account has been hacked, and I need help recovering it. The email address I have provided is the one which is originally associated with the account."

To my dismay, after entering this information, I was redirected to a chat page with what I believe to be a support bot, and not a real person. Shown below are the transcripts of the conversation I had with the bot.

This was the extent of the support I was able to get from Roblox, a totally useless chatbot that would end the conversation without helping me at all. I tried again several times to get support like this, but the bot was never helpful. This constitutes a serious lack of **error recovery** within Roblox's account system, which is the extent to which a system is able to help a user understand why a problem occurred and how to solve it. In this case, losing access to my account is a serious problem, and Roblox has not provided a way for me to recover from it. Another problem with this chatbot is the lack of **feedback**, which is how clearly a system is able to communicate what is happening and what the next steps are. The chatbot simply gave me vague responses that did not address my specific situation, linking me to resources I had already seen and making me wonder whether the system was processing my request at all.

In addition to these fruitless attempts to get the chatbot to help me, I emailed Roblox directly, which got me the following response from their automated reply service:

Note that the support page they linked me is the same one I was just using and prompted me to send Roblox an email in the first place. Not only does Roblox's chatbot support not work at all, Roblox has essentially blocked off all other means of getting support from an actual human. This support is ineffective because it does not provide any avenues for me to get the support I need and just wastes my time.

## Strengths of System
I'm sure that for simpler, less complex problems that users might have, the current support system probably works just fine. The support form had other categories of problems that the user could find help with in the dropdown menus, for example, there is a How To option, which presumably allows the user to ask how to do something. The AI chatbot might be effective for this task if the question is simple enough for the bot to answer. However in more complex situations such as mine, the chatbot completely fails to provide any assistance.
